## 3. OAuth permissions can now be designed around the task—not the whole app

- **Verified source/date:** Cloudflare, **2026-08-20 17:03 UTC**.[3]
- **What changed — fact:** Cloudflare OAuth clients can mark scopes as required or optional; users may decline optional scopes, and scope evaluation is limited to the capabilities requested in that authorization flow.[3]
- **Why it matters to Jet — interpretation:** This makes least-privilege agent access more testable. An agent may need read access for one task but write/deploy access for another; blanket authorization is now easier to challenge with concrete evidence.
