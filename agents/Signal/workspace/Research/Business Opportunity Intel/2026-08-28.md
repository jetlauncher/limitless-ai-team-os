## 1. The sellable education outcome is “AI quality plus human reasoning,” not AI literacy alone

- **Verified sources/date:** OpenAI randomized experiment and Google/Khan Academy classroom launch, **2026-08-27**.[1][2]
- **What changed — fact:** OpenAI reported a randomized experiment with more than 1,000 first-year students: ChatGPT access improved rubric-scored quality and coherence, while separate causal-reasoning training increased idea variety and explanation of why ideas might work or fail; the combined group showed both types of benefit. Separately, Khanmigo launched adaptive interactive math/science diagrams plus teacher-reviewed, editable, scorable AI-generated practice assignments.[1][2]
- **Why it matters to Jet — interpretation:** A credible Limitless education offer should not promise that AI makes learners “smarter.” It can sell a measurable two-layer outcome: AI-assisted production quality plus retained originality, causal reasoning, and teacher/facilitator control. This is stronger than a generic prompting workshop and gives institutional buyers a defensible assessment story.
- **Revenue action:** Add an **AI + Reasoning Lab** module to the next commercially approved workshop or institutional proposal: participants produce one AI-assisted answer, defend its assumptions and failure conditions, then revise it using a rubric that separately scores outcome quality, originality, causal logic, and human judgment.

## 3. AI-media provenance is becoming an audit line item

- **Verified sources/date:** AWS implementation blueprint, **2026-08-27**; EU Article 50 and transparency-code guidance currently applicable from **2026-08-02**.[4][5][6]
- **What changed — fact:** AWS published a reference architecture that embeds an imperceptible watermark at AI-audio synthesis, attaches signed C2PA provenance metadata through downstream transformations, stores a tamper-evident chain of custody, and recovers provenance when metadata is stripped. EU guidance states that Article 50 transparency obligations apply from August 2, 2026 and cover machine-readable marking/detection plus disclosure or labelling for specified AI-generated content.[4][5][6]
- **Why it matters to Jet — interpretation:** Clients using synthetic voice, avatars, ads, podcasts, or training media now need an operational answer to “Can you prove how this asset was generated and edited?” A disclosure sticker alone does not solve asset lineage, metadata loss, approval ownership, or cross-channel reuse.
- **Revenue action:** Add an optional **AI Content Provenance Readiness** screen to the AI Leverage Audit for media-heavy or EU-facing clients: generator inventory, disclosure owner, C2PA/watermark support, metadata-preservation test, approval log, and evidence-retention policy. Position it as operational readiness, not legal advice.
