### 3. Training Paradigm Shift: RLVR + GRPO + Synthetic Data = Agent training on a single GPU
NVIDIA and others now demonstrate that **Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR)** paired with **Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)** lets you train/specialize agents using clean synthetic data — all on a single GPU with 80% less VRAM than traditional methods. No critic model needed. The loop: model identifies its own weaknesses → generates training data → trains → repeats.

**→ Best Jet angle:** This is the technical backbone behind why "custom AI for your specific business" is becoming affordable and accessible for Thai SMEs. Content: "ตอนนี้เจ้าของธุรกิจไทยสามารถฝึก AI ให้ทำงานแบบเฉพาะทางได้ — ด้วยข้อมูลจริงของธุรกิจตัวเอง ไม่ใช่ข้อมูลสาธารณะ." Explains in plain language how synthetic data + RLVR makes custom agents feasible without massive budgets.
