## Top 3 Signals

### 1. RLVR / GRPO Replacing RLHF as the Post-Training Standard
Reinforcement Learning from Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) combined with Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) has become the dominant post-training stack, overtaking RLHF. Synthetic self-evolving data + verifiable-reward RL is now how models gain reasoning ability at lower cost.

**Why it matters:** The "reasoning edge" is increasingly a training-data advantage, not a parameter-count race. Small teams with good synthetic-data pipelines can outperform bigger ones.

→ **Best Jet angle:** Content series — *"The New Model Moat Isn't Parameters, It's Training Data."* Show Thai business owners why proprietary data quality (not AI tool choice) will determine who wins. Practical: how to build a synthetic-data workflow for your niche domain.

### 3. Synthetic Data for Small Models: SLMs Beat GPT-4o on Narrow Tasks
New research shows fine-tuning small language models (SLMs) with synthetic data can outperform GPT-4o on domain-specific tasks — sometimes with as few as ~10 well-crafted examples plus model-generated expansion.

**Why it matters:** The "bigger is better" narrative is dead. For Thai business owners who can't afford enterprise API costs, the signal is clear: narrow synthetic fine-tuning on a small model beats expensive general-purpose APIs.
