2. **AI cost is now an operating metric** (Signal: explicit prompt caching)
AI cost will quietly become a new margin leak.

Not because the tools are expensive.
Because teams keep sending the same context again and again.

This week, review your repeated prompts, SOPs, customer context, and reports.
If it repeats, it should be cached, templated, or turned into a workflow.

3. **Agents need a manager** (Signal: MCP authorization update)
Most AI agents fail for the same reason junior hires fail:

No clear scope.
No permissions.
No review rhythm.
No owner.

Before giving AI more autonomy, define what it can see, what it can change, and when a human must approve.
Autonomy without governance is just faster chaos.

4. **SOPs are the new AI fuel** (Signal: Claude Opus 5 on AWS)
Better models don’t remove the need for process.
They punish unclear process faster.

If your best employee holds the workflow in their head, AI cannot scale it.

Write the SOP.
Add examples.
Define the decision rule.
Then let AI help operate the system.
