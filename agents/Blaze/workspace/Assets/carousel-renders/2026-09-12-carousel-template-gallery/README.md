# Limitless Club Carousel Template Gallery

This gallery compares the carousel systems currently available to Blaze using one shared theme: **AI that buys back the business owner’s time**.

## Dedicated carousel skills

1. `carousel-creator`
   - General carousel architecture, writing, rendering, photo integration, contact-sheet QA, and multiple visual families.
2. `jet-business-carousel`
   - Thai-first 10-slide business / AI / mindset carousel system. Default production look: Midnight Luxe.
3. `grok-jet-voice-carousel`
   - Grok/xAI-native English carousel visuals grounded in Jet’s transcript voice. Best for short English copy and hand-drawn founder-education graphics.
4. `openai-image2-carousel-generation`
   - OpenAI gpt-image-2 / ChatGPT Image 2.0 art-directed carousel workflow. Best for visually rich premium concepts; exact Thai copy is overlaid with HTML/Chrome after generation.

## Reusable visual template families shown

1. **Simple Twitter / X**
   - Fast, sparse, text-first creator thought.
   - Best for: short Thai insights, direct observations, quote-style posts.

2. **HOW Bangkok / Founder Publisher**
   - Photo + lower-left cover headline; clean centered lesson cards inside the deck; photo interstitials for pacing.
   - Best for: workshop lessons, event recaps, founder stories.

3. **Founder Field Notes**
   - Tactile business notebook, paper fragments, stamps, annotations.
   - Best for: business scars, operating lessons, practical strategy notes.

4. **PP Neue Montreal Dark Editorial**
   - Swiss grid, dark operator-media feel, oversized typography and restrained metadata.
   - Best for: strategic frameworks and premium founder thought leadership.

5. **Gold / Black**
   - Black, warm gold, symbolic diagrams and numbered education.
   - Best for: AI news, tools, trend explainers, platform changes.

6. **Midnight Luxe First Batch**
   - Editorial serif + real Jet photo + minimal philosophy-led layout.
   - Best for: CEO mindset, founder principles, premium thought leadership.

7. **Midnight Luxe Notion Batch**
   - Operational cards, comparisons, systems, lists, blueprints.
   - Best for: workflows, AI operating systems, lead magnets.

8. **Hormozi Midnight Luxe**
   - Sparse centered contrarian claim with almost no decoration.
   - Best for: hard reframes and memorable business mental models.

9. **Grok Native Founder Education**
   - Dark navy, warm white, muted gold, hand-drawn diagrams.
   - Best for: English digital-teammate and workflow teaching.
   - Gallery image is a **local no-cost style mockup**. Production uses xAI/Grok and verifies provider metadata.

10. **OpenAI Image 2 Art-Directed**
   - Rich editorial imagery, premium abstract object, dramatic but restrained art direction.
   - Best for: high-impact hero covers and collectible premium decks.
   - Gallery image is a **local no-cost style mockup**. Production uses `gpt-image-2`, then exact Thai typography is repaired/overlaid locally.

## Recommendation

Use a compact four-style house system for most production:

- **Simple X** for speed and volume.
- **HOW Bangkok** for founder stories and workshop authority.
- **Midnight Luxe First Batch** for premium signature thought leadership.
- **Founder Field Notes** for distinctive, collectible teaching content.

Use **Grok** or **OpenAI Image 2** when the visual concept itself should carry the post and generation cost/time is justified.
