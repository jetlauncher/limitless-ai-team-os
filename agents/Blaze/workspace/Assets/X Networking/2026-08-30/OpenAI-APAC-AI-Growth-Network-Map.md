## Daily — 15 minutes

1. Read the private X list, not the general feed.
2. Save one post that connects to Jet’s real work.
3. Leave at most one substantive reply.
4. Record the person, topic, and next useful contribution.

## Weekly

- Publish **2 English field notes** from Thai business adoption.
- Publish **1 build artifact**: workflow, evaluation, diagram, or short demo.
- Leave **5 high-quality replies** across the priority accounts.
- Quote-post only when Jet adds original data, a counterexample, or an APAC implication.

## Relationship ladder

1. Follow and observe
2. Reply with a concrete field signal
3. Publish a related independent artifact
4. Reference their framework accurately
5. Continue the conversation naturally
6. DM only after repeated relevant interaction or a clearly useful reason
7. Offer data, a case study, an introduction, or a regional opportunity
8. Invite to a focused conversation—not “pick your brain”

## What not to do

- Do not mass-tag OpenAI employees.
- Do not paste generic AI-generated compliments.
- Do not immediately request a meeting.
- Do not call every experiment a breakthrough.
- Do not exaggerate SME outcomes.
- Do not send the same DM to multiple people.
- Do not turn every reply into a Limitless Club pitch.
