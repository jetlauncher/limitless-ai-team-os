# ICP Hot-Button Messaging — คุณณัฐ

Source: https://jedi-icp-hot-button-map-deploy.vercel.app/

## Durable messaging hierarchy
- Lead with the immediate implementation gap: `ปัญหาไม่ใช่วันเสาร์ที่เรียน แต่คือวันจันทร์ที่กลับไปทำงาน`.
- Reduce AI overwhelm: customers do not need 30 tools; they need a small, curated set tied to their real bottleneck.
- Frame outcomes as owner leverage: work removed from the founder, time returned, lighter teams, repeatable workflows, and support when implementation gets stuck.
- Use the ① frustration → ③ near-term result bridge in hooks and CTAs; close longer content with the ② fear → ④ identity/aspiration bridge.
- Preserve exact testimonial wording. Brand copy may frame the hot button, but customer quotes must remain source-traceable and unstrengthened.

## Primary ICP
คุณณัฐ: Thai business owner, age ~44, team ~11, revenue ~฿8M/month; feels trapped as the bottleneck, overwhelmed by AI choices, afraid another course will not survive Monday, and wants practical leverage without looking foolish or wasting more time.
