## Micro-Upgrades (YouTube Focus)

### Upgrade 1: Unify Thumbnail Color System → Bronze Accent Discipline
**Change:** Standardize ALL new thumbnails to use ONE accent color — the bronze (#94764A) from the brand palette. Replace the scattered blue electric glows with warm gold/bronje-toned highlights on product faces or text overlays.

**Why it increases luxury:** Blue neon/glow effects read as "tech startup" not "premium founder media." Bronze = limited, rare, expensive association. Think Rolls-Royce: bronze and black together signal heritage luxury, not SaaS promo. Consistent accent color across a 20+ video grid signals editorial discipline — the same reason Vogue never uses neon pink on its cover.

**Implementation:** Add a thumbnail style guide layer:
- Background: dark (#17181A or near-black photo)
- Accent text/logo highlight: #94764A bronze (use sparingly, max 5% of thumbnail area)
- Face photo always on right third Rule of thirds
- Brand watermark in top-right corner: small "Jedi" logo
- Remove all blue/purple electric glow effects

**Priority:** HIGH — most visible to new visitors. This creates immediate luxury first impression at brand-scale.

---

### Upgrade 2: Clean up Banner — Replace Red YouTube Button with Bronze CTA
**Change:** Swap the bright red YouTube "SUBSCRIBE +" button graphic on the banner for a bronze-toned ("#94764A") custom rectangle that matches the brand palette. Remove social media icons (IG, X, F) if they look cramped; keep only @JEDITRINUPAB as white text in bottom-right corner with a thin horizontal bronze divider line above it.

**Why it increases luxury:** The red YouTube button is a platform-native element pasted into the banner — it screams "I'm using the default template" not "I designed this." Bronze = intentional brand color. Red-to-bronze swap alone costs nothing but elevates the entire visual reading of premium intent.

**Implementation:**
- Recreate banner in Canva/Figma: keep existing crowd photos + Jet image
- Replace red button with #94764A rectangle with white text "SUBSCRIBE" (no bell icon)
- Bottom-right: thin bronze line + "@JEDITRINUPAB" in clean sans-serif (Inter or SF Pro light)
- Keep Thai headline text but increase its negative space surrounding area

**Priority:** HIGH — banner is the first visual real estate on the channel page. It's the business card; make it match the suit.

---

### Upgrade 3: Standardize Title Prefix System → Editorial Authority Cues
**Change:** Create a predictable, premium title prefix system for videos in these exact cases:
- Full tutorials → `[Masterclass]` instead of `[Full Course]` (sounds more exclusive)
- AI tool comparisons → no prefix needed — let the headline stand alone
- Quick tips (< 10 min) → `[Tool]` + tool name, e.g., `[Tool] Claude Fable 5`
- Business/brand philosophy → no prefix (clean, confid ent)

Additionally: pick one language path PER video. No mixing Thai headlines with English subtitles in thumbnails. If a thumbnail headline is in Thai, keep the YouTube title in Thai. If English, all-in on English.

**Why it increases luxury:** "[Full Course]" reads like a Udemy/Coursera commodity listing. "[Masterclass]" borrows from the highest-premium education brand. Consistent prefix system (not inconsistent mixing) creates visual predictability that signals editorial authority — like how The Economist never randomizes its headline formatting.

**Priority:** MEDIUM — affects perception over many videos, not a single-day change. Apply going forward.

### Upgrade 4: Add Gold/Subscriber Threshold Badge After Major Milestones
**Change:** When hitting subscriber milestones (150K, 200K), create a bronze-framed "milestone video" banner variant — dark background, thin bronze corner borders, gold accent text celebrating the number. This is only for milestone videos, not permanent.

**Why it increases luxury:** Milestone celebration frames are used by luxury brands (Rolex anniversary editions, Hermès special releases) to signal scarcity and prestige. Bronze/gold framed numbers on black = collector's edition energy. This turns subscriber growth into content events rather than just status updates.

**Implementation:** Only for 150K, 200K, 500K milestones:

- Big number in serif or high-weight typeface

**Priority:** LOW — milestone-dependent. Note the playbook for future use.
