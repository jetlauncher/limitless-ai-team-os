## Micro-Upgrades (Ranked by Luxury Impact)

### Upgrade 1: Kill Thumbnail Palette Chaos → Bronze-Only Accent System
**Change:** All new thumbnails must use ONE accent color (#94764A bronze). No blue, no orange gradients, no raw screenshots as backgrounds. Every thumbnail = dark BG + Jet portrait or scene image + ONE bronze-highlighted element (headline word, product name, or logo fragment).

**Why luxury:** 5 different colors in a row screams "I'm churning content" not "I'm curating wisdom." One color across an entire grid reads like a magazine — the visual equivalent of a fashion house's seasonal palette. Consistency = confidence = premium.

**Implementation:** Thumbnail rule card for Jet/AI:
1. Dark BG (#17181A or near-black photo)
2. Accent text highlight in #94764A (max 5% of canvas)
3. Jet on right third or product/scene center-weighted
4. Never stack brand logos (Apple, Google, etc.) — they dilute authority
5. No price numbers in large fonts

**Priority:** 🔴 HIGH

---

### Upgrade 2: Replace Red Subscribe Button → Bronze CTA Rectangle
**Change:** Remove the red YouTube "SUBSCRIBE" button overlay on the banner entirely. Replace with a thin #94764A bronze horizontal strip or rectangle containing white text: "WATCH + SUBSCRIBE" or simply let the native platform CTA handle it — which is actually stronger because it signals "not desperate for subs."

**Why luxury:** Red buttons are YouTube's template default energy. Bronze = curated brand language. More importantly, **removing** the banner CTA entirely (and letting the platform button do its job) is itself a luxury move — only brands confident in their authority need to beg. Hermès banners never have "Buy Now" stickers.

**Implementation:** Recreate banner: keep Jet photo + crowd inset + Thai mission text. Add thin bronze divider line near bottom. Place @JEDITRINUPAB after the line — white text, clean sans-serif (Inter Light or SF Pro). Zero CTA graphics.

**Priority:** 🔴 HIGH

### Upgrade 3: Refine IG Name Field → One-Line Authority Statement
**Change:** Change name field from `"Jedi | AI Growth Coach & Business Consultant"` to:
`"Jedi · AI Scale Leader · Forbes TH"`

Or the Thai-equivalent one-liner that fits within Instagram's character limits but reads cleaner. Remove "& Business Consultant" — "Consultant" is a commodity word; "Scale Leader" or similar signals authority at a higher tier.

**Why luxury:** The pipe `|` separator feels like early-stage Instagram formatting (pre-UX evolution). Luxury brands use middots `·` for subtle separation, not loud vertical bars. Cutting "Business Consultant" (a crowded, low-prestige category) in favor of stronger signals creates differentiation at a glance.

**Priority:** 🟡 MEDIUM

---

### Upgrade 4: IG Bio → Tiered Credibility Architecture
**Change:** Current bio reads as an information dump. Restructure to tier 1 (identity) > tier 2 (proof) > tier 3 (action):

Line 1: `AI Growth Coach for founders` (or Thai equivalent — identity)
Line 2: `Forbes TH · Exp.` (or strongest credential)  
Line 3: `2K+ CEOs trained →` (social proof as bridge)

**Why luxury:** Luxury copy always leads with identity, backs with proof, follows with action. Information-dump bios read like resumes. Tiered bios read like editorial — they give the eye a path instead of a wall.

**Priority:** 🟡 MEDIUM — requires IG session access to implement. Draft-ready.
