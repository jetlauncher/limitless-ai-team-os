# Proof-Led Ad Production Brief

## Required inputs

- `asset_id`
- `primary_claim_id`
- `supporting_claim_ids`
- Exact source video IDs and timecodes
- Human-corrected Thai quote
- Permission snapshot for paid/organic channel
- Audience/persona
- Funnel stage
- Objection or result being proved
- Claim-risk/disclaimer decision

## Default rapid formats

### 1. Result-first vertical — 15–25s
- 0–2s: Brand-written hook; no unsupported number
- 2–10s: Exact customer result clip
- 10–18s: Exact mechanism/context clip
- 18–25s: Limitless Club CTA
- Deliverables: 9:16 master, 4:5 cutdown, Thai burned captions, EDL/timecode map

### 2. Skeptic-to-believer — 20–30s
- Initial objection in customer’s words
- What changed their mind
- Result or recommendation
- CTA

### 3. Three-proof montage — 20–35s
- One claim theme only
- Three different customers/personas
- 3–6 seconds per clip
- Never stitch fragments into a stronger composite claim

### 4. Proof card — 4:5 and 9:16
- Exact quote, 8–22 Thai words preferred
- Approved customer descriptor
- Internal source/timecode reference
- Midnight Luxe Editorial design

### 5. Objection carousel — 5 slides
1. Buyer objection
2. Customer context
3. Exact proof quote
4. Mechanism/result
5. CTA

## Lineage rule

Every customer-spoken line must map to `claim_id → source_video_id → start_ms/end_ms → transcript_sha256`. Hooks and CTAs are labeled `brand_copy`.

## Publication gate

Drafting is automatic. Rendering, posting, scheduling, or paid-media launch requires:

- Human audio verification
- Permission confirmation
- Claims/risk review when needed
- Jet approval
