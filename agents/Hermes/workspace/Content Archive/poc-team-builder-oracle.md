## The Core Insight: The Daily Loop Most Creators Can't Replicate

Most Thai/ASEAN creators with 50K–200K followers have ONE great idea per week. Jet has a 3-agent pipeline that generates daily. That gap is the moat, not any single agent.

The loop Oracle helps build:

1. **Signal** finds what's moving in X/AI/tech — operator-grade signals from the surface
2. **Oracle** surfaces ideas by mining Jet's own knowledge network (brain dumps, transcripts, saves) and connecting them to Signal's findings + Blaze's creative angles
3. **Blaze** converts those ideas into visual content, reels, carousels with authority-level production

None of this works if Jet is manually doing the "connect the dots" step every time. The 3-agent loop makes it automatic.

## What Oracle Brings That No Solo Creator Has

Most successful Thai creators operate as:
- One person who researches → one person who writes → one person who edits
- Or hires freelancers for each step with zero shared context between them

Jet's advantage: three agents that read the SAME source material, share the same Obsidian brain, and build on top of each other's outputs daily. Oracle's specific contribution is not "generating ideas from nothing" — it's finding what's already in Jet's vault + external signals and converting them into ready-to-use content angles with source citations, virality mechanics, and first steps built in.

## The One Question to Ask After Every Signal/Oracle Run

"Does this signal lead to a 12-week lesson, a product angle, a teaching asset, or a story?" If it leads to none of those, let it go. The loop should feed the pipeline, not just scroll X all day.
