## Sweep Summary

This sweep points to a sharper operator thesis: AI advantage is moving into the boring control layers of business — who agents can access, what data guides recommendations, how workflows are evaluated, and where repetitive operational decisions can be delegated safely.

- Overlap note: Recurring themes remain: agent infrastructure, AI-mediated acquisition, and operational trust/QA. Newly interesting in this run: the better founder framing is shifting from “new AI features” to controllable systems — permissions, steering data, workflow ownership, and KPI loops.
