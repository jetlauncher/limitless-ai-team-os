## Top 3 Angles

### 1. Customer conversations are becoming the new operating dashboard

- Why it matters: Founders often treat support chats, sales calls, and feedback forms as scattered noise. The strongest operator signal is that AI can now turn this messy customer language into product priorities, retention risks, sales plays, and team SOPs — without waiting for a quarterly report.

- Recommended formats: Thai authority post, carousel: “From Conversations → Operating System”, Skool lesson on customer-signal SOPs, LinkedIn operator breakdown.

- Hook: Your best dashboard may not be a dashboard. It may be every customer conversation your team is ignoring.

- Hook: If AI only answers tickets, you are underusing it. The real win is learning from the customer faster than your competitors.

- Hook: Thai owners do not need more reports. They need a system that turns daily customer noise into Monday-morning decisions.

- Supporting point: AWS/Hapag-Lloyd uses Bedrock to transform customer feedback into actionable insights for digital customer-experience teams.

- Supporting point: Intercom frames support capacity as a revenue engine and customer-agent layer across ecommerce/sales.

- Supporting point: ElevenLabs/Klarna/Revolut signals show voice/support interactions becoming measurable workflows, not novelty demos.

- Supporting point: Founder move: create a weekly “customer intelligence loop” — collect conversations, tag reasons, extract objections, decide one SOP/product/sales change.

- Source signal: AWS ML Blog RSS; Intercom RSS; ElevenLabs Blog index.

- Jet personalization note: Strongest Jet fit: connect to Thai owners whose knowledge is trapped inside LINE chats, sales calls, and senior staff memory. Use Jet’s “business cannot scale on memory in a few people’s heads” / systems-over-superheroes theme and turn it into a practical customer-conversation SOP.

### 2. Agent access is the new delegation matrix

- Why it matters: As agents move from writing into coding, browsers, payments, cyber work, and account actions, the founder question becomes: what can this AI touch, spend, approve, and escalate? Treating AI like a tool is too loose; production agents need delegation rules like employees.

- Recommended formats: Thai founder post, carousel checklist: “Before agents touch money”, LinkedIn POV, Limitless Club lesson on AI approval matrices.

- Hook: A chatbot is a tool. An agent with access, budget, and browser control is an employee.

- Hook: Before you automate the work, automate the permission system.

- Hook: If your AI can touch customers, code, or money — it needs a manager, not just a prompt.

- Supporting point: OpenAI’s Codex safety framing emphasizes sandboxing, approvals, network policies, and agent-native telemetry.

- Supporting point: AWS AgentCore signals include payments with Coinbase/Stripe, OS-level browser actions, and identity on ECS.

- Supporting point: OpenAI GPT-5.5-Cyber Trusted Access shows high-capability AI is increasingly gated by verified roles and use cases.

- Supporting point: Founder move: map AI authority levels — observe, draft, recommend, execute with approval, execute within budget, escalate.

- Source signal: OpenAI RSS; AWS ML Blog RSS; The Decoder RSS.

- Jet personalization note: Use Jet’s AI-first leader and replace-don’t-add language: don’t add agents randomly; redesign the delegation system. Thai-owner example: quotation approval, ad budget changes, customer refund, purchase order, or LINE admin reply.

### 3. The best AI opportunities are the parts of expert work with an answer key

- Why it matters: The market keeps showing AI entering expert domains through checkable subproblems: seismic workflows, math proofs, code/security tests, reward functions, and BI questions. The lesson for founders is not “replace experts”; it is “decompose expertise into steps that can be verified.”

- Recommended formats: Thai authority post, carousel: “How to find AI-ready work”, workshop worksheet, LinkedIn thread.

- Hook: AI does not replace experts first. It eats the parts of expert work that can be checked.

- Hook: Stop asking “what can AI do?” Ask “which part of this work has an answer key?”

- Hook: The best AI workflow starts with one sentence: how will we know the answer is correct?

- Supporting point: AWS/Halliburton converts natural-language queries into executable seismic workflows and reports up to 95% workflow acceleration.

- Supporting point: AWS RLVR/GRPO post focuses on objectively verifiable rewards for math, code, and symbolic tasks.

- Supporting point: The Decoder reports GPT-5.5 Pro math-research progress and Mozilla’s agentic pipeline finding 271 Firefox vulnerabilities by building/running tests.

- Supporting point: Founder move: audit work by verification type — numbers, policy rules, customer acceptance, human approval, test case, money collected.

- Source signal: AWS ML Blog RSS; The Decoder RSS.

- Jet personalization note: Good Jet framing for “wisdom in noise”: leaders should not chase demos; they should identify one expert bottleneck in the company and design a testable workflow. Fits training teams and SOP creation.
