## Top 3 Angles

### 1. Your AI agent is only as good as the test it must pass

- Why it matters: The market is moving from demos to autonomous work, but capability is outpacing measurement. Founders cannot scale agents into sales, support, ads, code, or payments without defining pass/fail tests, logs, and review loops.

- Recommended formats: Thai authority post, CEO checklist carousel, internal team-training lesson, workshop module on AI QA

- Hook ideas: ถ้า AI ของคุณทำงานแทนคนได้ แต่คุณไม่มี test ว่ามันทำงานถูกหรือไม่ — คุณไม่ได้ scale ระบบ คุณกำลัง scale ความเสี่ยง / The bottleneck is no longer prompting. It is knowing when the AI is wrong before your customer notices. / Don’t hire an AI agent until you know what exam it must pass every week.

- Supporting points: The Decoder reports METR can barely measure Claude Mythos with current suites, while Palo Alto warns of autonomous attacker workflows.; AWS AgentCore quality optimization points to production traces, batch evaluations, and A/B tests as the operating loop for agents.; OpenAI Codex safety emphasizes sandboxing, approvals, network policy, and telemetry — evaluation and control are now part of deployment.

- Source signal: The Decoder May 10 METR/Palo Alto + sandbagging reports; AWS AgentCore Quality Optimization; OpenAI Codex safety RSS.

- Jet personalization note: Use Jet’s systems-over-superheroes line: the issue is not whether AI is smart, but whether the company has a repeatable system to test, approve, and improve the work before it touches customers or money.

### 2. Support is becoming the new revenue department

- Why it matters: AI can remove repetitive support load, but the real unlock is redeploying human capacity into consultative revenue, upsell, retention, and customer insight loops. This is practical for Thai owners scaling without adding headcount.

- Recommended formats: Thai founder post, sales/support SOP lesson, LinkedIn post, carousel: cost center → revenue engine

- Hook ideas: ถ้า AI ช่วยตอบคำถามซ้ำ ๆ ได้ งานของทีม support ไม่ควรหายไป — งานควรเลื่อนขึ้นไปใกล้ revenue มากขึ้น / The best AI support project is not fewer tickets. It is better conversations that create revenue. / Don’t ask only ‘how many agents can AI replace?’ Ask ‘what higher-value customer work can my team finally do?’

- Supporting points: Intercom says freed-up capacity from Fin supported a consultative support function and meaningful business results.; Fin for Sales/outcome pricing ties AI value to qualified leads; Fin for Ecommerce moves customer agents closer to checkout.; For operators, the workflow should define qualification, handoff, discount approval, CRM notes, and post-chat follow-up.

- Source signal: Intercom May 8 support-to-revenue, Fin for Sales outcome pricing, and May 7 Fin for Ecommerce RSS items.

- Jet personalization note: Connect to Thai owner pain: don’t replace people blindly; redesign the support/Sales SOP so AI handles repeated questions and humans handle trust, closing, retention, and customer learning.

### 3. Agents crossing the money line need CFO rules, not just tech rules

- Why it matters: AWS AgentCore Payments and outcome-priced AI sales tools show agents moving from advice into transactions. Once AI can trigger spend, discounts, checkouts, refunds, or vendor payments, founders need approval thresholds and balance-sheet discipline.

- Recommended formats: CFO-style Thai post, carousel, CEO memo, sales/revenue workflow checklist

- Hook ideas: วันที่ AI แตะเงินบริษัทได้ prompt ไม่พอแล้วครับ ต้องมี financial control / The moment an AI agent can pay, refund, discount, or buy, it becomes a finance process. / Before giving AI a credit card, decide the rules your best CFO would demand.

- Supporting points: AWS previews AgentCore Payments with Coinbase and Stripe, enabling agents to access and pay for services used.; Intercom’s outcome-pricing frame changes buying from seats to measurable qualified leads.; The founder risk is uncontrolled spend, wrong discounts, refund mistakes, vendor commitments, and unclear accountability.

- Source signal: AWS AgentCore Payments; Intercom outcome-based pricing for Fin for Sales; prior cost signals on GPT-5.5 usage economics.

- Jet personalization note: Tie to balance-sheet discipline and sales workflow: create a Thai SME checklist for spend caps, discount authority, refund rules, human approvals, CRM logging, and weekly ROI review.
