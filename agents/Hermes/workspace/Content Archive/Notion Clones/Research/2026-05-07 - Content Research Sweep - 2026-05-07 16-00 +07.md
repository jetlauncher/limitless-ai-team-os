This sweep intentionally avoids simply repeating the 14:05 themes of proof-backed workflows, ChatGPT ads, and OS-level agent actions. The fresher pattern is that AI advantage is becoming a management and placement problem: who can train AI-native teams, put AI into live customer moments, and prove outputs with citations, memory sources, traces, and feedback loops.
