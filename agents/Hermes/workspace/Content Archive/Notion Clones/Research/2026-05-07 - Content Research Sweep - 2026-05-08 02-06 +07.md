# Sweep Summary

This sweep shows AI moving from assistant interfaces into accountable business roles: agents that speak to customers, agents that can transact, and internal AI systems that save measurable time. The operator opportunity is to define authority, QA, escalation, security, and workflow ownership before scaling automation.

- Overlap note: Recent sweeps repeatedly covered AI as operating infrastructure, default work surfaces, trust/security, and AI-mediated marketing. Newly interesting this run: agents are gaining economic agency through payments, voice is becoming a production service channel, and frontier-firm adoption is now visible through daily time savings and Codex/workflow integration—not just abstract enterprise enthusiasm.
