## Top 3 Angles

### 1. Outcome-priced AI agents turn support and sales into a P&L workflow

Why it matters: Intercom’s same-day cluster around support as revenue, Fin for Sales outcome pricing, and Fin for Ecommerce points to a buying shift: founders will stop asking “how many seats?” and start asking “which revenue event does this agent own?”

Recommended formats: Thai authority post, carousel, workshop lesson, LinkedIn post for founders, revenue-ops checklist

Hook ideas:

- ถ้า AI agent ของคุณตอบลูกค้าได้ แต่ยังวัด revenue ไม่ได้ — คุณยังไม่ได้สร้าง agent คุณแค่สร้าง chat widget

- The next AI sales hire may not have a salary. It may have outcome pricing.

- Support is not a department anymore. In an AI-first company, it is a revenue workflow.

Supporting points:

- Intercom published signals on support becoming a revenue engine, outcome-based pricing for Fin for Sales, and ecommerce customer agents.

- The founder implication is measurement design: what counts as qualified lead, assisted conversion, saved churn, upsell, or resolved objection?

- For Thai SMEs, this reframes AI from cost-saving bot to sales/service system that can be trained, audited, and tied to cash flow.

Source signal: Intercom RSS, May 7–8: support-to-revenue, Fin for Sales outcome pricing, Fin for Ecommerce.

Jet personalization note: Use Jet’s “replace don’t just add” theme: don’t add AI on top of bad customer service; redesign the customer conversation, objection handling, lead qualification, approval limits, and revenue dashboard first.

### 2. AI marketing advantage is shifting from button-clicking to business-input quality

Why it matters: Google’s AI bidding/budgeting, AI creative, data-to-decisions, and Small Brief signals all point in one direction: AI can operate campaigns faster, but it still needs clean goals, offers, measurement, exclusions, and a sharp customer brief.

Recommended formats: Carousel, Thai post, ad-operator training module, “Monday audit” checklist, X/LinkedIn thread

Hook ideas:

- AI ไม่ได้ทำให้คนทำการตลาดขี้เกียจได้ แต่มันลงโทษ brief ที่ไม่ชัดเร็วขึ้น

- Your ad account is no longer a control panel. It is a steering system.

- Bad offer + bad measurement + AI bidding = faster waste.

Supporting points:

- Google is pushing AI bidding/budget tools in Search and Shopping, plus AI creative guidance and data-decision messaging.

- The Small Brief shows AI helping small businesses, but the name reveals the real leverage: brief quality.

- Founder task: clarify margin, target customers, disallowed claims, product feed, conversion events, and learning loop before increasing budget.

Source signal: Google Blog / Ads & Commerce, May 5–8: bidding/budgeting, creative quality, data-to-decisions, Small Brief.

Jet personalization note: Anchor in Thai owner context: a restaurant, clinic, education business, or ecommerce owner should stop asking “ยิงแอดยังไงให้ถูก” and start building the offer/customer-data/measurement SOP that AI campaigns can actually steer from.

### 3. Model capability is outrunning measurement — founders need job-based evals before rollout

Why it matters: The fresh risk signal is not just “AI is powerful.” It is that labs and benchmarkers are struggling to measure capability while agents gain coding, cyber, payments, and computer-control primitives. Operators need workflow-specific tests, not vibes.

Recommended formats: Founder checklist, carousel, internal training lesson, executive memo, Thai camera script

Hook ideas:

- อย่า roll out AI เพราะ demo ดูฉลาด ให้ roll out เพราะมันผ่าน test งานจริงของบริษัทคุณ

- The question is no longer “is the model smart?” It is “can we prove it is safe enough for this job?”

- If you cannot measure the job, you cannot delegate it to AI.

Supporting points:

- OpenAI’s Codex safety framing emphasizes sandboxing, approvals, network policy, and telemetry.

- AWS highlights verifiable rewards where outputs can be objectively checked.

- The Decoder reports METR’s current tests barely cover a new Claude capability range and flags autonomous attacker risk; treat as a measurement-warning signal.

Source signal: OpenAI RSS on Codex safety; AWS ML Blog on verifiable rewards/GRPO; The Decoder, May 10 on METR/cyber-agent measurement gaps.

Jet personalization note: Connect to Jet’s “systems over superheroes” and team-training angle: create 20 real company tasks, expected answers/actions, failure examples, and approval rules before asking staff to “use AI more.”

### 4. Agent payments mean AI is crossing the money line

Recommended formats: Carousel, finance SOP checklist, founder memo, short reel

Hook ideas:

- The scariest AI permission is not access to your files. It is access to your money.

- Before your agent can buy, your company needs a buying policy.

- AI payments are coming. Your approval matrix cannot stay in someone’s head.

Supporting points:

- AWS announced AgentCore Payments with Coinbase and Stripe.

- Google donated Agent Payments Protocol to FIDO Alliance, pointing to standards for secure agentic payments.

- Thai SMEs often keep approval logic informal; this becomes dangerous once agents can trigger purchases/subscriptions.

Source signal: AWS ML Blog, May 7; Google Blog / Pay, Apr 28.

Jet personalization note: Tie to balance-sheet discipline: AI agents should have spending envelopes, supplier whitelists, invoice matching, human approval thresholds, and logs.

### 5. Expert SOPs are becoming executable interfaces

Why it matters: Halliburton’s natural-language seismic workflow signal is a clean proof that the best enterprise AI opportunities are boring expert workflows: ask in plain language, generate a workflow, and use documentation Q&A to reduce dependency on a few specialists.

Recommended formats: Thai post, course lesson, SOP workshop, founder carousel

Hook ideas:

- ถ้า SOP ของคุณยังอยู่ในหัว senior staff คนเดียว AI จะช่วย scale ไม่ได้

- The AI opportunity is not replacing experts. It is making expert workflow executable by the team.

- Natural language is becoming the front door to specialist operations.

Supporting points:

- AWS/Halliburton built a POC converting natural-language queries into executable seismic workflows plus Q&A.

- This pattern applies to sales quoting, service troubleshooting, inventory planning, claims, and onboarding.

- Founder task: map the decision tree and examples before buying the agent tool.

Source signal: AWS ML Blog, May 8: Halliburton seismic workflow creation with Bedrock and generative AI.

Jet personalization note: Use Jet’s recurring line that business cannot scale on memory in a few people’s heads; show a Thai operations team turning senior-staff know-how into SOP + examples + test cases.

### 6. AI unit economics are becoming a leadership skill, not a technical footnote

Why it matters: Rising model costs, GPU capacity planning, and enterprise AI usage growth make AI spend a gross-margin issue. Founders need routing, model tiers, caching, usage budgets, and ROI per workflow.

Recommended formats: Founder memo, carousel, CFO-style worksheet, Thai post

Hook ideas:

- AI ที่เก่งขึ้นแต่แพงขึ้น อาจทำให้ margin ธุรกิจคุณแย่ลงถ้าไม่มี design

- Your AI strategy needs a cost model before it needs another prompt library.

- Treat model choice like inventory: right quality, right cost, right workflow.

Supporting points:

- The Decoder reports GPT-5.5 real-usage costs 49–92% more depending on input length; secondary-source signal.

- AWS highlights short-term GPU capacity planning for ML workloads.

- For SMEs, the lesson is not GPU buying; it is separating premium reasoning tasks from cheap routine tasks.

Source signal: The Decoder, May 10; AWS ML Blog, May 7.

Jet personalization note: Anchor to balance-sheet discipline: AI-first leader means knowing cost per lead, cost per ticket, cost per generated document, and when not to use the most expensive model.
