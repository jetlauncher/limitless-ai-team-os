- Recommended formats: Thai authority post, LinkedIn founder POV, carousel: “From survey data to AI interviews”, workshop lesson on customer intelligence systems.

- Hook ideas: The next research advantage is not a bigger dashboard. It is 10,000 conversations you can actually analyze. / Most founders still use AI to write content. - Supporting points: Anthropic describes nearly 81,000 Claude user interviews across 159 countries and 70 languages as a large multilingual qualitative study. - Why it matters: The Decoder’s GPT-5.5 Pro math signal, AWS verifiable rewards, and fakeable reasoning traces all point to one truth: AI work scales best where the company can verify outputs independently of the model’s explanation.

- Recommended formats: Carousel, Thai long-form, LinkedIn “stop trusting reasoning traces” POV, workshop exercise.

- Hook ideas: Stop asking whether AI sounds smart. Ask whether your business can verify the answer. / The best AI workflows have an answer key. The worst ones have vibes. / Reasoning traces can be fake. Outcomes are harder to fake.
