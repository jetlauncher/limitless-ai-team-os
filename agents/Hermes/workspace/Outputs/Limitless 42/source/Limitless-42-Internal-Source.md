# Limitless 42 — Internal Program Source

**Dates:** September 1–October 12, 2026  
**Length:** 42 days / six weeks  
**Status:** Owner-review draft; HTML is the user-facing artifact.

## Promise
In six weeks, members finish one meaningful mission while building the discipline, energy, relationships, personal systems, and responsible AI leverage needed to keep growing without sacrificing family, faith, or soul.

## Pricing
- Founding cohort: ฿7,900 or 2 × ฿4,200 (฿8,400 total)
- Cap: 100
- Guarantee: 7-Day Activation Guarantee: complete at least five daily check-ins and the first weekly review; if the route still feels unclear, request a full refund by Day 8.

## Daily scorecard
- **Ground:** 10 minutes of prayer, reflection, gratitude, or intentional stillness
- **Body:** 20 minutes of movement plus the week’s energy anchor
- **Build:** 45 focused minutes on the member’s six-week mission
- **Leverage:** 15 minutes improving a system, skill, delegation, or appropriate AI use
- **Proof:** Post the day’s evidence and one honest sentence before sleep

## Graduation
- At least 34 of 42 daily check-ins submitted
- At least 5 of 6 weekly reviews completed
- One meaningful October 12 finish line completed or honestly closed with evidence
- One trusted AI-assisted workflow documented with a human quality gate
- One useful asset, act of service, or courageous ask completed
- A signed next-30-day covenant shared with a witness

## Complete route

### Day 01 — September 1, 2026 — Choose the six-week finish line
- **Week:** Truth and Direction / Face
- **Mission:** Write one outcome you can complete or clearly verify by October 12. It must matter, fit the available time, and require real growth—not fantasy capacity.
- **Leverage:** Use AI only to find vague words, assumptions, and missing measures. You make the final choice.
- **Proof:** Post the one-sentence finish line, success measure, and why it matters.

### Day 02 — September 2, 2026 — Score the whole life
- **Week:** Truth and Direction / Simplify
- **Mission:** Rate Body, Work, Money, Relationships, Inner Life, and Joy from 1–10. Add one sentence of evidence for every score.
- **Leverage:** Create a simple six-domain table or chart. Do not let AI choose the scores.
- **Proof:** Share the lowest domain and one truth you have been avoiding.

### Day 03 — September 3, 2026 — Audit where the week went
- **Week:** Truth and Direction / Practice
- **Mission:** Reconstruct the last seven days in 30-minute blocks. Mark time as Create, Maintain, Connect, Recover, or Leak.
- **Leverage:** Ask AI to group the blocks and surface the three largest mismatches between stated priorities and actual time.
- **Proof:** Post one time leak you will reclaim this week.

### Day 04 — September 4, 2026 — Interrogate the contradiction
- **Week:** Truth and Direction / Leverage
- **Mission:** Complete this sentence five times: ‘I say I want ___, but my calendar shows ___.’ Answer without defending yourself.
- **Leverage:** Use AI as a direct but compassionate interviewer for five follow-up questions; answer from evidence.
- **Proof:** Share the contradiction that will become your focus.

### Day 05 — September 5, 2026 — Delete a borrowed goal
- **Week:** Truth and Direction / Ship
- **Mission:** Cross out one goal, obligation, or identity you are carrying mainly to impress, please, or keep up with others.
- **Leverage:** No AI today. The work is discernment, not optimization.
- **Proof:** Post what you are releasing and what space it creates.

### Day 06 — September 6, 2026 — Make the promise visible
- **Week:** Truth and Direction / Connect
- **Mission:** Tell one trusted person your October 12 finish line, daily commitment, and how you want them to challenge you.
- **Leverage:** Draft the message yourself. AI may shorten it, but it cannot remove the vulnerability.
- **Proof:** Post ‘Promise made’ plus the name or role of your witness.

### Day 07 — September 7, 2026 — Run the first honest review

- **Leverage:** Ask AI to summarize patterns only after you complete the reflection yourself.

### Day 08 — September 8, 2026 — Set the sleep and movement floor

### Day 09 — September 9, 2026 — Turn off the attention casino
