# Limitless Superhero Universe — Current Team Roster

Date: 2026-08-16
Art direction: original superhero universe with 1960s Silver Age ensemble-comic energy, modern premium founder aesthetics, bold ink, halftone, dynamic silhouettes, and no copied Marvel/X-Men characters, costumes, logos, insignia, or exact compositions.

## Selection system

Every individual sheet contains three designs, left to right:

- **A — First Issue:** bright Silver Age comic energy; clean shapes, primary/secondary colors, bold ink.
- **B — Midnight Ops:** contemporary black/navy tactical suit with refined metallic role-color accents.
- **C — Cosmic Legacy:** elevated mythic/cosmic form with dramatic cape, aura, or role-based energy construct.

## Infrastructure presence

**Qron — The Time Ring** is not presented as a humanoid hero. It appears in ensemble art as the orbital timing system above the team: a subtle mechanical halo representing recurring schedules and automations.

## Team visual rules

- Original characters only; no recognizable Marvel/X-Men costumes, insignia, faces, poses, or logos.
- Cohesive collection: bold black ink, tactile halftone, paper grain, strong silhouettes, sophisticated anatomy.
- Thai-future identity through disciplined navy/gold geometry and founder-system motifs, not stereotypical or religious imagery.
- No readable generated text inside the artwork. Names and selection labels are added locally afterward.
- Heroic, intelligent, premium, warm; avoid generic cyberpunk robots and cheap neon.
